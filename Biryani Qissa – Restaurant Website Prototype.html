Our Story

Hyderabad is a city of long dinners, loud families and plates passed down the table. Biryani Qissa begins there, with a simple idea: rice, spice and patience can make an ordinary evening feel like an occasion.

Each dish is imagined as a small story, from the first lift of the lid to the last spoonful shared across the table. This copy is placeholder text written for a teaching prototype; a real brand would replace it with its own founding story.

Ordering experience

Three simple steps

A short, clear journey keeps customers confident. Try it yourself by adding dishes to the demo cart above.

  1. Choose your meal

    Browse the menu and add your favourites to the demo cart.

  2. Place an enquiry

    Send your selection as a message, so the team can check availability and delivery to your area.

  3. Confirm your order

    The restaurant replies with the final price and timing. You confirm, and only then is the order placed.
